Digital Ecosystem Demo

A premium builder brand world for support, cruising and community.

This demo shows how a yacht builder could connect practical support, cruising intelligence, events, owner stories and brand updates into one calm digital experience.

01 / Fix

Support, troubleshooting and maintenance flow.

Fault reporting, troubleshooting, support requests, maintenance history, parts used, cost tracking and owner/builder support flow should be powered by Onboard rather than copied into a separate Fix app.

02 / Plan

Cruising intelligence, trips and yacht admin.

World map intelligence sits beside rally planning, itineraries, budgets, pre-departure checklists, destination notes, useful contacts and trip admin. The map layer should be powered by World Map rather than duplicated.

Demo principle

Build once as MYH core, then wrap or theme for the ecosystem demo.

Fix points to Onboard and Plan points to World Map. The ecosystem should frame, theme, link to or demonstrate core products rather than forking them into duplicate applications.
